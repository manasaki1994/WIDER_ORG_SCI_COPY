Questions

When the tool is in doubt it asks instead of guessing: a screening answer that could not be used, three reads that all disagree, a DOI or seed paper that finds no abstract, a proposal the model could not produce, and while the query is refined, the questions the evidence raises (a name to add, a noise term, a seed not retrieved), each with its default and consequence. Each answer is applied at once and the change is recorded with the question.

When a question comes up

Answered and dismissed
Decisions

Every answer, accepted default and dismissal of a refinement question, with its round (the synonyms version) and author. Later synonyms proposals and mining prompts list them under "Decisions from the researcher"; quotes from seed papers are kept as references, never as text.

History

Every change you make by hand is kept here: labels, adjudications, duplicate decisions, the status of concepts and synonyms, notes, tags, answered questions and every settings or protocol edit. Undo puts back what was there before, but only when nothing has changed it since; an undo is a change too, so it can be undone. A freeze is final, and saved versions of concepts and synonyms never change (restore an older one as a new version on the Concepts and synonyms page). Ctrl/⌘+Z outside a text field undoes your last change.

Settings and data

Settings of the open project: the AI models and which one each step uses, the data policies, the review protocol and the doubt settings. Models are added and chosen on the Model for query creation page and the Ranking page; this page edits and removes them and checks that they answer.

Review protocol

The defaults reproduce the original review and are tuned for recall first: a record is excluded only when the model can show, with a verbatim quote, that the construct is absent or the study is unrelated; everything uncertain goes to maybe for a human. The sampling bands put a census on the top of the ranking and a thinning probability sample below it, so the recall estimate has a confidence interval rather than a guess. Two independent passes and a blind third read resolve disagreements by majority. Open the panel below only when a reviewer, a budget, or a methods section asks for something different; every setting carries its own explanation.

Model for query creation

Review plan

Project folder

Five tasks set up the review. Do them in any order and come back to change any answer; a task with a question from WIDER says so on its card.

    Plan summary

    What the project file now records. Every later stage reads it; the run specs and the report carry its hash.

    ‹ Review plan

    Research question

    The question and the notes are shown to the model in every proposal and every screening prompt, in your words. Write the question as a reader of the review would understand it; use the notes for scope you already know you want in or out.

    ‹ Review plan

    Seed papers

    Development seed papers are used while you build; validation seed papers are held back and checked once, so a good split needs papers in both.

    ‹ Review plan

    Prior reviews

    Earlier reviews of the construct, or of a neighbouring one, give a second check: their included studies should turn up in your exports (coverage check) and their reference lists and citing works can be chained into the corpus. Add a reference export, paste DOIs or titles, or drop the review's PDF; the reference list is extracted on this computer.

    ‹ Review plan

    Databases

    Pick the databases and their order. Give the platform you know best the development role in the table below; when no role is set, the first platform is the development platform.

    ‹ Review plan

    Find more seed papers optional

    The reference lists of your development seed papers and the works citing them can name papers you have not found yet. Nothing becomes a seed paper until you add it.

    Concepts and synonyms

    Open a tab (cells, codebook, synonyms, anchors, near_miss) and press Propose with LLM, or type your own; save it as a new version, approve the version later stages should use, and freeze when done. Seed papers ground every proposal.

          Where screening stands
          Loading…
          
    
          
    
          
      
          
      
          
      
          
        
          Start screening
          
        
          The model reads each record's title and abstract and answers include, maybe or exclude. One pass is one reading of every record. Protocol A/B + third read reads every record twice, independently, and a third time where the two disagree; the majority decides. Second screen of maybes reads the maybes again.
          
        
          
          
        
          
        
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
        
          
        
          Record limit: caps the new model calls at this many per pass, as only the first records of the scope are screened and records already screened are not called again. A protocol run whose passes are finished still decides every record, without new calls.
