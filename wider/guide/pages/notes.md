# Notes and tags

## Page role
- every note and tag on the records

## What to do
1. Search the notes and tags, or filter by tag.
2. Open a record to read or change its notes.

## Why it matters
- Notes keep the reasons behind decisions next to the records they concern.

## What you need
- Notes or tags added from a record's drawer.

## Done when
- Nothing to finish: use it when you need it.
