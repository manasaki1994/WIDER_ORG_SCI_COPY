# Missing abstracts

## What to do
1. Read how many records have no abstract or a short one.
2. Press **Fetch next 100** to look up abstracts in the sources the protocol names, in that order.
3. Press **Retry failed lookups** for lookups that failed before.

## Why it matters
- Ranking and screening read the abstract; a record without one is judged on its title alone, so it is easily ranked low
  or set aside.

## What you need
- Online lookups allowed in Settings and data: only DOIs and titles leave this computer.

## Done when
- The lookups have run, or no record has a missing or thin abstract left to look up; the records still short are
  marked thin and are screened with that in mind.

## Watch out
- Nothing already present is overwritten, and every lookup is logged.
