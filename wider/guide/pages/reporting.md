# PRISMA reporting

## Page role
- the PRISMA 2020 and PRISMA-S checklists, what WIDER recorded for each item and your own text for the paper

## What to do
1. Choose the protocol and result set, then open the items marked "Researcher input needed" or "To check".
2. Write your text for an item, or save WIDER's draft, and say where the item is reported in your paper.
3. On the Flow diagram tab, enter the counts WIDER cannot know, then press **Export checklists**.

## Why it matters
- PRISMA asks how the search and the screening were done; WIDER fills what it recorded, so you write only the rest, and your text stays apart from its records.

## What you need
- A screened result set.

## Done when
- Every item is documented, has your text or is marked not applicable, and the exported checklists are filled in.
