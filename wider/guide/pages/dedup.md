# Deduplication

## What to do
1. Check the list of uploads: every database you searched should be there.
2. Press **Run deduplication** (after the first run this button reads **Apply decisions and check again**).
3. Some pairs look alike but WIDER is not sure. Decide each one: **Duplicates, keep A** or **Duplicates, keep B** (the kept record's title, authors, year,
   journal and DOI stand for the paper), **Not duplicates** or **Unsure**.
4. Press **Apply decisions and check again** to apply your decisions.

## Why it matters
- The same paper often comes back from several databases. This step finds those duplicates so each paper is
  screened once, and the PRISMA counts of records found and removed are final once every pair is decided and the
  decisions are applied.

## What you need
- The exports of every database you searched, uploaded on Search results.
- Your name, set once beside "Working as" in the top bar, so every decision on a pair is signed and can be undone.

## Done when
- The run is done and no pair waits for your decision.

## Watch out
- A decision without a name is refused; Change beside "Working as" in the top bar sets it.
- The PRISMA numbers are provisional until every pair is decided and deduplication has run again with the decisions.
  The diagrams, the exports and the Results page say so while they are.
