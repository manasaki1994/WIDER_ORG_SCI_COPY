# History

## Page role
- every manual change, signed, with undo

## What to do
1. Read the changes, newest first; choose a kind under **Show** to see one kind only.
2. Press **Undo my last change**, or **Undo** on a change that can still be undone.

## Why it matters
- Every manual change is journalled, so it can be traced to who made it and undone without losing later work.

## What you need
- Nothing beyond the project.

## Done when
- Nothing to finish: use it when you need it.
