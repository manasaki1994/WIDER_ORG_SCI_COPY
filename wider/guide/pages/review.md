# Screening review

## Page role
- the records the screening left to a person

## What to do
1. Open each record the screening left to a person and decide it.
2. Press **Draw quality-control sample** to check a sample of the model's decisions by hand.

## Why it matters
- Records the model could not settle need a person's decision before the result set is final.

## What you need
- A screening round with records left to a person.

## Done when
- No record waits for a person.
