# Review plan

## What to do
1. Open each task card in turn: **Research question**, **Seed papers**, **Prior reviews**, **Databases**, and the
   optional **Find more seed papers**. Each card shows its state and what is still missing.
2. On a task page, enter what it asks for, press its save button, then follow the **Next task** link at the end of the page, or **‹ Review plan** at the top.
3. Answer the questions from WIDER in the window at the bottom right; a card marked **Waiting for you** has one.

## Why it matters
- Every later step is checked against these inputs: the concepts come from your question and seed papers, and the
  validation seed papers test the search once, honestly.

## What you need
- The research question in a sentence or two.
- A handful of papers you already know belong in the review, by DOI or title.

## Done when
- The research question, the seed papers, the prior reviews and the databases are done; the line under the cards
  then reads "Ready for the construct stage".

## Watch out
- Seed papers are split into a development and a validation set; the validation papers are held back until the
  search is frozen or the uploads are marked final.
- Find more seed papers and the online lookups send DOIs or titles to OpenAlex only after you agree on that page.
