# Concepts and synonyms

## What to do
1. Open a tab (cells, codebook, synonyms, anchors, near_miss) and press **Propose with LLM** to draft it from your
   question and seed papers, or type your own under **Show as text**.
2. Read each proposal, edit it under **Show as text** and press **Save as new version**.
3. Press **Approve** on the concepts, the synonyms, the anchor sentences and the codebook you want later steps to use.

## Why it matters
- A broad construct is reviewed well only when its vocabulary and boundaries are written down before searching.
  Ranking needs the anchor sentences and screening needs the codebook, so this step is never optional.

## What you need
- The research question and seed papers from the Review plan.
- A model for query creation, or your own text typed in.

## Done when
- The concepts and the synonyms are approved.

## Watch out
- Every version is kept; restore an older one with **Restore as new version** rather than editing history.
