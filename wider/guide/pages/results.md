# Results dashboard

## Page role
- the decisions of one result set, with their counts and exports

## What to do
1. Choose the result set and read the counts per decision and route.
2. Press **Export** for the records of a preset, or **Every set at once**.

## Why it matters
- The counts here match the exports, so what goes into a reference manager can be traced to its decisions.

## What you need
- A screened result set.

## Done when
- The records you need are exported.
