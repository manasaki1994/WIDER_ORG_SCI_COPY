# Questions

## Page role
- the points where WIDER is in doubt and asks you

## What to do
1. Read each open question and its options.
2. Choose an option and press **Save answer**, or **Dismiss** a question that does not apply.

## Why it matters
- WIDER does not guess where a choice is yours; each answer is recorded and can be undone.

## What you need
- Nothing beyond the project.

## Done when
- No question is open.
