# Recall and report

## What to do
1. Press **Estimate recall** to compare the screening with the human labels of the probability sample.
2. Press **Build report**. The PRISMA 2020 diagram, PRISMA-S and the checklist are filled in on **PRISMA reporting**.
3. Press **Draft from the project** for the methods paragraph, edit it and press **Save methods paragraph**.
4. Press **Build the package** for the replication package, or **Export every record** for the plain corpus export.

## Why it matters
- The claim a review makes is that it found nearly all of the relevant work; this step puts a number and an
  interval on it.

## What you need
- A screened corpus and a labelled probability sample.

## Done when
- The report is written.

## Watch out
- Shared files write researchers by the attribution setting under **Names in shared files**.
