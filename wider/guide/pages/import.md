# Search results

## What to do
1. Press **Upload and check** for the export of each database (RIS, CSV or TSV, XLS or XLSX, or BibTeX).
2. Read the sanity verdict of each file and the seed paper coverage per database.
3. Press **These uploads are final** when every database is uploaded.

## Why it matters
- Each record keeps its source file, so the PRISMA counts and the coverage of your seed papers can be traced.

## What you need
- The exports of your searches, one per database.

## Done when
- At least one export is imported.

## Watch out
- A file is imported once, by its content; the validation seed papers are reported only once the uploads are final
  or the search is frozen.
