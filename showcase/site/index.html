WIDERprototype

A literature review tool for broad constructs, where the same idea is studied under many names

Some topics are hard to review not because there is little written about them, but because it is spread across many fields and vocabularies. WIDER leads a researcher through such a review step by step: the model proposes, reads and quotes; the researcher decides; and every step is recorded.

Try the demo yourself: the same invented review in your browser, with a guided tour in chapters. Nothing you do there is sent anywhere.

One review, ten steps

Each step has a page in the app and a command for scripts. A step opens when the one before it is done.

  1. 01

    Question and seed papers

    The papers you already know guide the search and later test whether it found them.

  2. 02

    Construct

    Concepts, anchors, a codebook and synonyms. The model proposes; you edit, approve and freeze each version.

  3. 03

    Search strings

    The approved synonyms become a search string in each database's own syntax.

  4. 04

    Import

    Database exports are read as they are, with a check on which seed papers each search found.

  5. 05

    Duplicates

    Clear matches merge on their own; the unclear pairs are shown side by side for you to decide.

  6. 06

    Missing abstracts

    Filled from open scholarly indexes when you allow it; nothing is overwritten.

  7. 07

    Ranking

    Records are sorted by closeness to the construct and never filtered out.

  8. 08

    Human sample

    A stratified sample that a person labels blind, without seeing the model's decision.

  9. 09

    Screening

    Two independent model reads and a tie-break, each decision backed by verbatim quotes.

  10. 10

    Recall and report

    A recall estimate with a confidence interval, the PRISMA flow and a methods report.

Who does what

The split is the same on every page.

You decide

Approve the construct, settle unclear duplicates, label the blind sample and sign each decision with your name. Nothing moves on without you.

WIDER proposes and records

Drafts synonyms, ranks and screens with quotes, and stores every model call with its prompt, so the review can be replayed offline.

Runs on your computer

A local model is enough, and records do not have to leave the computer. Cloud models, such as the one whose screening the video replays, can be added; their keys are read from the environment only.

WIDER is a research prototype. The papers, authors and records in the video are invented demo data.
